## In-Store Technology Is Redefining Operations, Customer Experience, and Profitability

Retail is entering a new era. According to a global survey conducted in June 2025 by Bain & Company and AI To Market among 65 executives from major international retailers, technology adoption is no longer an experiment but the new norm for in-store operations and customer engagement. The findings show an industry investing heavily in digitalization, aiming for faster returns on investment and reshaping the face of the "store of the future."

### Key Findings from the Bain & Company - AI To Market Report

Customer experience comes first, with three customer-centric priorities:

- Better customer engagement (45%)
- Price integrity (53%)
- Product availability (56%)

Already widespread adoption:

- 33% have introduced "scan-and-go."
- 44% use RFID.
- 55% have deployed self-checkout at scale.
- 60% of retailers have adopted electronic shelf labels (ESLs)

"Product availability, price integrity, and customer engagement are the three top priorities for retailers today."

### Why It Matters

The shift is not just about operational efficiency. Stores are evolving into true hybrid hubs of commerce, media, and data:

- New revenue streams: retail media networks and in-store digital advertising are transforming static displays into dynamic, data-driven assets, generating new monetization opportunities.
- Operational gains: automation optimizes inventory management, price updates, and e-commerce order preparation, freeing up time for higher-value tasks.
- Enhanced experiences: AI-driven tools, from smart shelves to geolocation, enable faster and smoother customer journeys.
- Faster ROI: nearly 70% of retailers now estimate they recoup their technology investments in less than three years.

### Building Future-Proof Stores

The report highlights that while retailers test an average of five technologies simultaneously, obstacles remain:

- Slow decision-making (43%)
- Regulatory constraints (40%)
- High costs (32%)

Success relies on a clear roadmap:

- Start from customer and employee pain points.
- Engage the entire organization.
- Think in terms of platforms rather than standalone tools.
- Invest in both technology and team upskilling.
- Integrate financial performance across online and offline.

### Next Steps

- Richer data feeds the next wave of retail innovation.
- More customers generate more data.
- Better prices attract more customers.
- Lower costs enable better prices.
- Better systems reduce costs.
- New in-store technologies improve infrastructure and systems.
