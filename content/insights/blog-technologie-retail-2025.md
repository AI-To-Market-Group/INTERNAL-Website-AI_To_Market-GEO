## The Physical Store Has Never Been More Digital

The results from the Bain & Company x AI To Market report are clear: the physical store is entering an era of profound transformation. Here are the key takeaways for retail professionals.

### 1. Tech Investment Has Become Priority #1

More than half of the executives surveyed place in-store technology at the top of their strategic priorities. It's no longer a "nice to have" but a competitive imperative.

![Investment infographic]()

### 2. The Customer at the Center of Everything

The top three priorities cited by retailers are all focused on customer experience:

- **Product availability**: the customer wants to find what they're looking for
- **Price integrity**: trust and transparency
- **Engagement**: an experience that makes people want to come back

### 3. Measurable ROI

The days when in-store tech was a cost center are over. Nearly 70% of retailers recoup their investments in less than three years.

> Connected stores are no longer a futuristic concept — they are today's reality.

### What This Means for Your Strategy

If you're a retail decision-maker, three concrete actions emerge from this report:

- **Think platform**, not standalone tool
- **Invest in training** as much as in technology
- **Measure the impact** on customer experience, not just on costs
