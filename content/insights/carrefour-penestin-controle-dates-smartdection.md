### How Carrefour Pénestin Reduced Date Checking Time by 80%

- Solutions: Smartdetection • Flash Evo
- Store area: 1,200 m²

Faster short-date management that guarantees zero expired products on shelves.

Before adopting Smartway and AI To Market solutions, Carrefour Pénestin managed its short-dated products entirely manually. Each product had to be checked one by one across all aisles, meaning over 5,000 products to inspect every day.

### Record Time Savings with Smartdetection and Flash Evo

With Smartdetection, employees are guided via a PDA, which tells them directly which products to check.

Smartdetection Flash Evo combines Smartway and AI To Market's electronic shelf labels. Flashing electronic labels instantly indicate which products need attention.

Result:

- Before Smartway: 2h–2h30
- With Smartdetection: 1h
- With Flash Evo: 30 minutes
- ✅ 0 expired products on shelves

### An Intuitive Solution, Adopted in Just a Few Days

Installation was frictionless, and employees quickly integrated the tool into their daily routine.
