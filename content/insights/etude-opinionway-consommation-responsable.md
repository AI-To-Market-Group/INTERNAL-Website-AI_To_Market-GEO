#### RESPONSIBLE CONSUMPTION:

#### THE FRENCH READY TO ACT, BUT AT WHAT PRICE?

OPINIONWAY STUDY FOR AI TO MARKET: "THE FRENCH & RESPONSIBLE CONSUMPTION"

OpinionWay and AI To Market conducted a study on the relationship between French consumers and responsible consumption. The finding: commitment is present, but comes up against trade-offs between environmental engagement, product accessibility, and economic constraints.

IN BRIEF

STRONG AWARENESS AND FIRM COMMITMENT…

- The three most cited commitments: accepting less packaging for deliveries (91%), recycling electronic devices (89%), repairing rather than buying a new device (86%).
- 98% say they are willing to make at least one effort toward more environmentally friendly consumption.
- 77% of French people consider themselves responsible consumers.

… BUT LIMITED BY PRICE

- On average, French consumers are willing to accept a price increase of 10.5% for "Made in France" products, 9.7% for local products, and 7.1% for organic products.

PRICE: THE #1 CRITERION FOR PURCHASING DECISIONS FOR 3 IN 4 FRENCH CONSUMERS

- 75% of French people rank price as the main purchasing criterion.
