Localized assortment planning is the key to customer loyalty and revenue growth.

By leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), retailers are transforming their product mix strategies, ensuring hyper-personalization and optimized profitability across all channels and at every point of sale, through real-time data analytics.

## Why Product Assortment Is Now Retailers' Secret Weapon

#### Customer Loyalty: The Link Between Assortment and Personalization

The fastest way to build customer loyalty is through localized assortment planning. Using artificial intelligence and advanced data analytics, omnichannel retailers can optimize their assortments to drive sales and strengthen loyalty.

#### Revenue Growth: How Localized Assortments Maximize Profitability

According to a McKinsey & Company report, a well-adjusted product mix can increase sales by 2 to 5% and improve gross margins by 5 to 10%.

## What Challenges Do Retailers Face Without AI and Machine Learning?

Retailers are overwhelmed by the growing volume of big data generated by customer interactions, supply chain activities, and digital channels. This data is often stored in separate silos, making it difficult to access actionable insights and optimize demand forecasting.

## How AI and Machine Learning Are Revolutionizing Product Assortment in Retail

Generative AI solutions are designed to automatically and rapidly process immense volumes of unstructured customer and inventory data. AI analyzes — and anticipates — evolving purchasing behaviors, enabling retailers to dynamically optimize their product assortments and adapt them locally.
