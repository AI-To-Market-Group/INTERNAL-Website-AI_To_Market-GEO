Is the physical store dead? Quite the opposite.

According to the latest **Bain & Company x AI To Market** report, **60% of retail executives** place in-store technology at the top of their priorities.

The numbers speak for themselves:

- **70%** recoup their tech investments in less than 3 years
- **55%** have deployed self-checkout at scale
- **60%** use electronic shelf labels

The store of tomorrow is a **hub of data, commerce, and media**. Those who invest today won't just survive — they'll lead the way.

And you, where are you in the digitalization of your stores?
