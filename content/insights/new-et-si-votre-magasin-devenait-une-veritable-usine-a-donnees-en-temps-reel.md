What if your store became a true real-time data factory?  

The "Intelligent Store" initiative by DM and Fusion is revolutionizing physical retail through massive deployment of IoT and AI technologies across more than 2,000 European points of sale. Result: every aisle is connected, measurable, and optimizable on a daily basis.  

Here are 5 concrete lessons for succeeding in your in-store digital transformation:  

• Don't let technical constraints hold back your ambitions anymore: prioritize intelligent infrastructure, particularly with solutions like Edgesense, which eliminate the limitations of traditional electronic shelf labels.  
• Transform every aisle into an active data point, capable of automating inventory and order management, freeing your teams to focus on the customer experience.  
• Optimize your omnichannel flows by integrating AI to streamline click & collect order preparation and guarantee product availability.  
• Make internal collaboration and innovation a living culture by involving all teams (IT, operations, marketing) in an agile approach.  
• Adopt an "AI Native" vision: start today integrating real-time data collection and analysis to anticipate future developments and improve your performance.  

The success of DM & Fusion proves that a successful digital transformation relies as much on technology as on organizational agility and the quality of the tech partnership.
