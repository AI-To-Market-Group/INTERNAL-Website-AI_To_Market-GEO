The smart store is no longer optional, it's a necessity to reinvent physical retail. 🚀

The partnership between DM, a European drugstore giant, and Fusion, a retail tech expert, provides a concrete example of this transformation through:

• Daily AI adoption via platforms like EdgeSense for a 360° view of the store  
• The combination of IoT, AI, and Digital Twin to anticipate stockouts and optimize the customer experience  
• Click & collect optimization with light indicators and stock geolocation to reduce errors and delays  
• A strong focus on change management: training, empowering, and breaking down internal silos  
• Choosing a committed technology partner capable of long-term support, with scalable solutions  

This digital transformation doesn't rely solely on technologies, but on close collaboration between technical innovation and human management.

For retail decision-makers, the stakes are clear: getting the entire internal ecosystem on board with this journey and adopting a pragmatic strategy to accelerate the physical digitalization of stores.

The smart store is the answer to boosting operational performance, customer satisfaction, and agility in the face of rising omnichannel commerce.
