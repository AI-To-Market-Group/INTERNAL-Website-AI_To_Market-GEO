Meta’s data centers are more than tech hubs—they’re engines driving American innovation, jobs, and sustainability.

Here’s how Meta is making a tangible impact through its AI-optimized data centers:

• **Powering innovation:** Meta fully funds energy needs and collaborates with utilities to upgrade infrastructure years in advance, supporting new power and transmission projects with transparency through annual energy reports.  
• **Creating jobs:** From construction to long-term operations, these centers generate skilled jobs and supply-chain activity, bolstered by partnerships with local training programs that prepare the workforce for tech and trade roles.  
• **Championing sustainability:** A robust water stewardship plan reduces operational water use, invests in restoration projects, and supports community water infrastructure—committing to transparent sustainability reporting.  
• **Investing in communities:** Funding local schools, nonprofits, digital skills initiatives, and offering targeted support for households and small businesses through dedicated grant programs ensures that benefits extend beyond the data center walls.

Meta’s approach embodies a responsible-growth strategy that integrates technological advancement with economic and environmental stewardship at the local level. It’s a model showing how strategic infrastructure investments can fuel AI innovation while uplifting American communities.
