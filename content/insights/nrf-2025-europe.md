📍 Paris Expo Porte de Versailles – Halls 4 & 6  
📅 September 16–18, 2025

AI To Market is delighted to participate in the first edition of NRF 2025: Retail's Big Show Europe, a major retail event organized by the NRF and Comexposium, taking place for the very first time in Paris.

### Why It Matters

This historic event marks the merger between the legacy of Paris Retail Week and the international influence of the NRF, under the theme "Retail Together." The goal: to bring together North American and European innovation around AI, omnichannel strategies, unified commerce, logistics, sustainability, and data-driven transformation.

### What AI To Market Will Showcase

📍 Visit us at booth #D136 to discover how we help retailers:

- Optimize shelf management through our category optimization and shelf analytics solutions.
- Enhance the in-store experience with real-time digital technologies that promote traceability and efficiency.
- Activate customer engagement with retail media tools and data-driven insights.
- Foster a more sustainable approach through intelligent food waste management and traceability systems.

### Our Strategic Objective

Participating in this first European edition allows AI To Market to:

- Meet directly with hundreds of retail decision-makers looking for advanced solutions for their stores.
- Highlight our expertise in retail digitalization, intelligent shelf analytics, and planogram excellence.
- Demonstrate our concrete impact through customer case studies from over 60 countries.
