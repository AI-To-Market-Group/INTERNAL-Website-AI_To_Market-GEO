## In-Store Inventory Management: Why Real-Time Control Defines Modern Retail Success

41% of consumers check product availability online before visiting a store. When that information is missing, inaccurate, or outdated, they simply don't go.

## What Is Real-Time Inventory Management?

Inventory management involves tracking and controlling the quantity, cost, and location of products at all times, to help retailers know what to order and when.

## Why Real-Time Inventory Management Is Essential

- Immediate updates: sales, returns, restocking, transfers.
- Improved availability: fewer stockouts.
- Demand forecasting: less overstock/understock.

## How Real-Time Management Reduces Stockouts

- Automated replenishment triggers.
- Optimized safety stock.
- Predictive inventory analytics.

## The Central Role of Electronic Shelf Labels (ESL) in Real-Time Inventory Management

ESLs play a decisive role: real-time tracking, system integration, error reduction, and better execution.

## Real-World Cases: Higher-Performing Retailers Through Real-Time Management

Retailers that have adopted these systems report:

- 30 to 50% fewer stockouts,
- +20% inventory accuracy,
- +18% customer satisfaction.
